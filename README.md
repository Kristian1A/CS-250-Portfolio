CS-250 Module Eight Journal

How do I interpret user needs and implement them into a program? How does creating user stories help with this?

I interpret user needs by understanding what the customer wants the program to do and what features would be most useful. User stories help because they explain the needs of the user in a simple way and give developers a better idea of what they need to create. In the SNHU Travel project, the requirements changed to focus on wellness and detox travel. Using Agile practices helped the team adjust the project to meet those needs.

How do I approach developing programs? What Agile processes do I hope to incorporate into my future development work?

When developing programs, I think it is important to understand the requirements, plan the work, test the program, and make improvements when needed. I learned that Agile allows developers to make changes throughout the project instead of waiting until the end. In my future development work, I hope to use Scrum practices such as sprint planning, daily communication, sprint reviews, and retrospectives. These practices can help me stay organized, find problems earlier, and improve my work over time.

What does it mean to be a good team member in software development?

Being a good team member means communicating with others, listening to feedback, completing assigned tasks, and helping the team reach its goals. It is also important to be open to changes and willing to learn from mistakes. During software development, team members need to work together and share ideas to create a successful product. I think being dependable and respectful helps make teamwork more effective.
